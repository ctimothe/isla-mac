## islandcn

Isla's island motion (`Sources/IslaKit/UI/IslandMotion.swift`) and the
geometry of its split carry measurements and formulas from islandcn, commit
`0011a41ea4882f3818caf35c98f3ee46b80c4a2c` from
<https://github.com/opencoredev/islandcn>. Its glyphs
(`Sources/IslaKit/UI/IslandGlyph.swift`, `GlyphRecipe.swift`) follow
islandcn's glyph recipes and renderer (`components/islandcn/glyphs.tsx`) and
include its hand-drawn car and cloud paths. islandcn is a React library; the
recipes and the renderer were re-expressed in Swift.
