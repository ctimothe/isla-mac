## islandcn

Isla's island motion (`Sources/IslaKit/UI/IslandMotion.swift`) and the
geometry of its split carry measurements and formulas from islandcn, commit
`0011a41ea4882f3818caf35c98f3ee46b80c4a2c` from
<https://github.com/opencoredev/islandcn>. No islandcn source code is
included: it is a React library, and the values and geometry were re-expressed
in Swift.
