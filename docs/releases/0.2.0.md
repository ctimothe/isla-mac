## Install

1. Download `Isla-0.2.0.dmg` below, open it and drag **Isla** to **Applications**.
2. The build is not notarized. Remove the download quarantine once:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Isla.app
   ```

3. Open Isla and click the notch.

Updating from 0.1.0: quit Isla from Settings first, then replace the app.

## What's new

**The island**
- Opens on a click. Hovering only brightens it. **Open on Hover** in Settings brings the old behaviour back.
- When nothing is playing it rests hidden inside the notch. A pause folds it into the notch and playback brings it back out.
- **Music Only** keeps films and videos off the island.
- A first-run welcome explains where Isla lives and how to quit it.

**Music and lyrics**
- Time-synced lyrics from your own LRC files and, optionally, from LRCLIB. There is a full lyrics page (⌥⌘L), and you can click a line to jump to it.
- A lock-screen player with lyrics, volume and audio output. It can be Transparent, Tinted or Solid, in two sizes.
- Liked Songs with a connected Spotify account.
- Skips, film hand-offs and same-album covers behave correctly.

**Shelf and clipboard**
- Screenshots and screen recordings appear on the Shelf, including ones taken on the lock screen.
- Shelf items are dated and listed newest first.

**Translate**
- Sixteen languages with automatic detection and a swap button.
- Translation runs on the Mac with Apple's Translation framework or Apple Intelligence. Uzbek and Kazakh use **Translate Online**, which is off by default.

**Everything else**
- New tab icons. Tabs change on a click.
- Shortcuts, Spotlight and Siri can read the current track and lyric, and set the lyric delay.
- Honours Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast.
