## What's new

- The island shows timers, recording, downloads and battery alerts, and any of them can wait beside the song as a bubble
- A new song shows under the notch the way the volume pop-up does: title, cover, artist and the playing bars
- Every icon is new, drawn from Hugeicons as islandcn draws them; AirPods, Beats, HomePod and Macs keep Apple's own shapes
- The island moves on the iPhone's own springs, one for each kind of change
- When another app records the microphone or a camera, the island says which app, with Mute and Quit
- The island can follow a download and notice an AirDrop
- A timer and a stopwatch live on the island
- The lyric stage and the lock card stand still between their lines, so they cost less at rest

## Install

Already running a release after 0.3.0? Isla offers this one on the island by itself: press **Install**.

New to Isla, or on 0.3.0 or earlier:

1. Download `Isla-0.5.0.dmg` below, open it and drag **Isla** to **Applications**.
2. This build is not notarized yet, so remove the download quarantine once:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Isla.app
   ```

3. Open Isla from Applications.
