## What's new

**A redesigned island that updates itself.** From this release on, Isla checks for a new version once an hour and offers it on the island: press **Install** and it replaces itself and relaunches. Every change to Isla reaches you this way.

**The island**
- The island is 300 pt wide, grows out of the notch, carries a thin outline on every wallpaper, and shows one activity at a time, ranked by importance.
- Files on the Shelf wait as a bubble beside the island while music plays.
- The headphones alert and an opt-in Volume and Brightness pop-up appear as expanded alerts. The pop-up needs Accessibility.
- Right-click the island for Clipboard, Shelf, Translate, Lyrics, Settings and Quit.

**The open panel**
- There are four pages, Music, Shelf, Clipboard and Translate, with no tabs and no rail. Swipe sideways or click the dots underneath to move between them. ⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab turn the pages while the panel has the keyboard.
- ⌃⌥⌘V opens the clipboard from any app.
- The player has a lyric band, a volume slider and an output menu. Lyrics sit in a column beside the player, can grow into a tall page, and can show a translated line under each line.
- The Clipboard reads each copy as what it is, keeps pictures and pins, and searches.
- The Shelf works like Finder's icon view, with a Delete key and two drop wells.
- Translate shows a pair bar, a source card and an answer you can hear, with history.
- Settings is a sidebar of ten sections, opened from the gear on every page.
- The lock screen rests as a banner that grows into the card.

**Fixes**
- Many fixes to clicks, the keyboard, the pointer and folding.
- Faster swipes, and an outline drawn off the main thread.
- The volume keys answer even while the app is busy.

## Install

Already on a release after 0.3.0? Isla offers this one on the island by itself.

New to Isla, or on 0.3.0 or earlier (0.3.0 cannot install updates itself, so this is the last time you do it by hand):

1. Download `Isla-0.4.0.dmg` below, open it and drag **Isla** to **Applications**. On 0.3.0, quit Isla from Settings first.
2. This build is not notarized yet, so remove the download quarantine once:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Isla.app
   ```

3. Open Isla and click the notch.

Isla now checks GitHub for updates once an hour, sending only Isla's version. Turn it off in **Settings → About → Check for Updates Automatically**. Nothing else leaves your Mac unless you turn it on.
