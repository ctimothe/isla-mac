## Install

1. Download `Isla-0.3.0.dmg` below, open it and drag **Isla** to **Applications**.
2. The build is not notarized. Remove the download quarantine once:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Isla.app
   ```

3. Open Isla and click the notch.

Updating from 0.2.0: quit Isla from Settings first, then replace the app. macOS treats each Isla update as a new app until Isla is signed with a Developer ID, so it asks once more for the folders and players Isla had access to (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, and Automation for Music and Spotify).

**Lyric Delay now does what its label says.** Before, a positive delay moved lines earlier; now a positive delay shows them later, and negative earlier. Your saved delay and your per-song corrections are converted the first time 0.3.0 runs, so every song lands exactly where it did.

## What's new

**Lyrics**
- **The next line shows a beat before it is sung.** The island's one-line lyric turns to the next line a little early so you can read it before you sing it. **Settings → Music → Show next line early**: Auto (the default; longer lines get up to 0.5 s, shorter ones about 0.35 s), 0.3 s, 0.5 s or Off. The highlight and the word sweep stay exactly on the voice.
- A line goes dark when its voice ends instead of staying lit through an instrumental, and a long instrumental gets its own marker.
- Every common LRC variant now reads: files with a byte-order mark, stacked or unknown tags, section labels, and word-timed lines. Before, one odd tag could refuse a whole file, and LRCLIB cached that refusal for two weeks.
- Lyrics that do not fit the recording's length are skipped for a better match.
- Lyric Delay moves in 0.05 s steps and can be tuned while you watch the line.
- **Lyrics timed for a longer version of the song are flagged.** Some songs are streamed as a shorter edit than the one the lyrics were timed for, which leaves every line running late. The lyrics page now says so, and **right-click the line being sung → Sync Here** lines the whole song up in one step (the per-song correction now reaches ±45 s, up from ±1.5 s).
- Lyric timers fire strictly on time (about 0.07 ms late at the median), the clock no longer jumps when the speed changes, and the island, the lyrics page and the lock card always agree on which line is current.
- A live stream says "No lyrics for live audio" instead of claiming lyrics are off. Local lyric files are followed when you move them and found in subfolders.

**Music and the lock screen**
- Music and Spotify without Now Playing now show shuffle, repeat and the heart, keep the right clock after a skip, and no longer raise an unprompted Automation dialog at launch.
- Lock card: the sung line wraps instead of being cut off, text stays readable over a pale cover, the volume bar keeps your balance and shows and lifts mute, a stream shows no dead seek bar, and with nothing playing the card takes no clicks.
- **Liked Songs works again with Spotify's new library endpoints** (the old ones were retired). Sign-in problems now say what went wrong.
- Album covers are held at 512 px, and the media helper no longer crashes on a malformed answer.

**Shelf and Clipboard**
- Shelf cards on an ejected disk stay and read "Disk not connected" instead of vanishing. Picked cards get an action bar, dragging out copies unless you hold ⌘, and AirDrop says why when it cannot send.
- Screenshots reach the Shelf on a Mac set to any language. Clipboard and Shelf fade where a list is cut off. **Delete These Files** empties the Shelf and its size at once.
- Clipboard reads only what your privacy switches allow and survives macOS pasteboard privacy. The cover now hides how many items there are and what kind.

**Translate**
- The privacy cover hides the translation as well, and no longer blinds your typing. Pasting a huge text is capped (20,000 characters, 4,800 online) and a partial online answer says so.
- The shortcut tells you when the clipboard holds no text. The default target language follows your Mac's language, and Ukrainian is told apart from Russian.

**Accessibility and keyboard**
- VoiceOver finds one **Isla** button, the open panel closes from its header, and Shelf cards, Clipboard rows and Settings switches are named with their actions. The charger, headphones, a new track, a skip, a copy and a refusal are announced. VoiceOver stays inside the lock card's output picker.
- Escape closes a panel opened from the keyboard, and so does switching to another app. Editing keys follow your keyboard layout and Caps Lock, ⇧⌘C copies the translation, and shortcuts show the key your layout prints.
- Reduce Motion starts and stops at rest and fades where it used to cut.

**Settings and system**
- Privacy comes second in Settings, About and Quit sit in a footer, and Launch at Login says when macOS wants your approval.
- A second copy of Isla hands over to the one already running. Show Isla and reopening Isla open the panel without closing it, and commands do nothing while the display sleeps.
- Online lookups, translations and covers keep no web cache and no cookies. The update check validates the release page before offering a download.
- Closing the panel fades the contents away before the edge reaches them, and dragging the lock-card volume no longer floods the system with writes.

## Known

- Isla still needs a Developer ID to be notarized, which is why the download needs the `xattr` command above.
- The lyric lead is unchanged at 0.20 s; use Lyric Delay if your speakers or headphones run late.
- Everything in this release is covered by over a thousand unit tests and the release checks, but not every behaviour could be checked on every Mac. If something looks wrong, **Settings → Copy Diagnostics** and **Report a Problem…**.
