# Island build plan

Approved 2026-10-11 by the CEO after an adversarial Opus review (verdict: approve with amendments). Five Opus planners produced 126 test-first tasks across lyrics, media and system, Shelf and privacy, accessibility and shell, and visual and Translate; the reviewer found 32 problems (2 blockers, 19 major, 11 minor), listed below with their fixes, and produced the 30-batch build schedule. Machine-readable plan: `2026-10-11-island-build-plan.json`. Task ids are prefixed by group: ly/ lyrics, ms/ media-system, sp/ shelf-privacy, ax/ a11y-shell, vt/ visual-translate. The 17 round-3 findings (K-001 to K-017) are folded into tasks by the reviewer's amendments; one new task, ms/MS-26, closes K-003.

## How it is built

Each batch is at most four tasks in separate git worktrees, built test-first by Opus engineers; the CEO reads every diff, merges in the listed order with merge commits onto `feat/island-next-level`, and runs the full unit suite plus the localization, branding and provenance gates before the next batch starts. P1 findings land first (batches 1 to 7). Tasks that need an owner or a physical Mac are marked and held.
