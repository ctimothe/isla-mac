# Handoff: Batch 22, the stage and lock card stand still (2026-11-05)

## What changes for users

Between lyric lines, the lyric stage and the lock card no longer redraw ten
times a second. Their progress bars and the banner's new ring now move on
Core Animation, the system's own animation engine, which needs no redraw.
In the idle-cost lab, the lock card between lines used 0.90–1.36 % CPU with
this change. The open stage between lines uses 1.44–2.15 %, about 1 point
more than the card (see "The stage's last 1 %" below). Nothing looks
different on screen.

## How to ship and roll back

Ship: in the worktree, run `./scripts/check` with the sandbox off. If it is
green, merge into `main` with a merge commit (`git merge --no-ff
feat/batch-22-stage-lock-card-off-tick`) and push `main`. CI
(`.github/workflows/release.yml`) versions, signs and releases it, and every
island offers it within the hour.

Roll back: revert the merge commit on `main` (`git revert -m 1 <merge>`) and
push. CI releases the revert as a newer version. On your own Mac, publish a
build of `main` with `bash Scripts/publish-dev-update.sh`.
